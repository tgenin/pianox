Note Pitch Guesser

Train your ear: a random note is played and you have to find it on the piano. Pick a range, hit New Note to start, then click the key you think matches. Your attempts and history are tracked so you can see your progress.

Play with your mouse, your computer keyboard (A/Q SDFGHJKL + W/Z E TYU OP) or your MIDI keyboard.
